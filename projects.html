Automating the Weekly C-Suite Dashboard

📅 Alteryx, Insights & Analytics Internship — Summer 2026 🛠️ Alteryx Designer, Tableau, SQL, AppScript

The Business Problem

Leadership relied on a weekly dashboard to track company performance, but building it was a manual process that took roughly 3 hours every week — pulling data from multiple sources, cleaning it, and reformatting it before it could even reach Tableau. That manual process was also a source of data accuracy issues, since any copy/paste or formatting slip could throw off the numbers leadership was making decisions on.

My Role & What I Did

I owned the end-to-end redesign of the dashboard pipeline. I built automated Alteryx workflows to blend, clean, and structure data from multiple source systems into a single, reliable dataset, then used Alteryx Designer and Tableau together to fully automate the weekly dashboard build. I also wrote a Google AppScript automation to keep a Google Sheet synchronized with the executive dashboard, so updates on one side flowed through without manual re-entry.

Challenges I Overcame

The source data came from multiple systems that weren't designed to talk to each other, so getting a single clean dataset required careful blending logic in Alteryx rather than a simple merge. Keeping the Google Sheet and dashboard in sync was another challenge — without automation, updates on one side easily drifted out of sync with the other, which is exactly the kind of manual error the project was meant to eliminate.

Artifacts

Due to the sensitivity of internal company data, the live workflow and dashboard can't be shared publicly, but I'm happy to walk through the approach in an interview.

Business Value / Impact

Cut dashboard production time from 3 hours to 5 minutes per week — a roughly 97% reduction in manual effort — while also improving data accuracy and reliability. Freed up significant analyst time that had been spent on manual data prep, and gave leadership a dashboard they could trust was current and correct every week.
