More About Me

The stuff that doesn't fit on a resume, but makes me, me.

Leadership & Community Involvement

Member of Pi Kappa Phi Fraternity (2023–Present). Co-founder and Co-leader of my high school's Operation Smile Club (2019–21), which I helped build from the ground up. I've also volunteered on service trips to Argentina, the Dominican Republic, and Hawaii (2018–23), and with Loaves & Fishes and the Boys & Girls Club (2017–19).

Athletics

Treasurer and Captain of DU Club Soccer (2024–Present), where I handle both team finances and on-field leadership. Previously Captain of Varsity Soccer at St. Anne's-Belfield (2021–22), and a member of Varsity Basketball and Lacrosse there (2018–22). I also played club soccer with the International Development Academy during my semester in Valencia, Spain.

Outside of Work

I've studied abroad twice — in Sevilla and Valencia, Spain — and I'm conversational in Spanish as a result. Between managing kitchens, driving for DoorDash, and captaining soccer teams, I've picked up a habit of staying useful in whatever room I'm in, whether that's with a spreadsheet or a scoreboard.
